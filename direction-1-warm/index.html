Dr. Kung’s local insight

Is it a cavity, or “Cedar Fever”?

Living in North Texas comes with unique challenges for your mouth.

Every year between December and February, we see dozens of patients rush in convinced they have a dying tooth in the upper back of their mouth. The real culprit? Mountain Cedar pollen.

Your upper molar roots sit right below your maxillary sinuses. When Cedar Fever inflames those sinuses, the pressure pushes down on the nerves of your teeth and mimics a toothache.

Pro tip: if your upper teeth hurt on both sides and you are congested, it might be allergies. If the pain is isolated to one specific tooth, call us at (972) 964-3800. That can be an infection needing urgent care.

Common questions

Questions about our Plano dental office

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?

Absolutely. We offer different levels of sedation to help our patients feel comfortable and relaxed, including nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation. If you are afraid of visiting the dentist, you have absolutely nothing to be ashamed of.

How much does it cost to visit a dentist in Plano without insurance?

Costs vary depending on the service, and our in-house adult wellness plan is $34 a month and the child plan is $29. We offer complimentary consultations where we can give you transparent pricing for what you actually need. New patients are always welcome.

Do you speak languages other than English?

Yes! At Vitality Dental, we speak your language, including English, Japanese, Chinese, and Spanish. Dr. Kung is fluent in Mandarin, and our team is trained to ensure every patient feels understood and empowered on their oral health journey.

What should I do if I have a dental emergency on the weekend?

If it is life-threatening, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room. That includes uncontrolled bleeding, swelling that affects your breathing or swallowing, or a facial injury.

For dental pain, a knocked-out tooth or a lost crown, call our office at (972) 964-3800. We prioritize emergency cases and offer same-day appointments whenever possible. Office hours are Monday to Friday, 7AM to 3PM.

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes. New patients and emergency appointments are always welcome. We have gone out of our way to make the office a place you can be comfortable in, with short wait times and a family-like atmosphere.
